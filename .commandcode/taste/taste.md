# Taste

## Communication
- Communicates in Vietnamese and expects explanations, findings, and recommendations delivered in Vietnamese. Confidence: 0.7

## Design & Visuals
- Prefers map/building illustrations in a consistent 3/4 isometric perspective (facade, door, windows, and roof visible), applied across all stage themes including city and mountain scenes, rather than mixing top-down rooftop-only views. Confidence: 0.75
- When a visual theme looks bad, prefers replacing it with an entirely different theme instead of incrementally polishing the existing one. Confidence: 0.65
- Prefers a mobile-game visual language for lesson roadmap markers: topic-relevant symbols/icons instead of numeric codes like "02", drawn as flat solid accent-colored shapes with no white border/inner ring. Confidence: 0.6
- Expects illustrated scenes to obey ground/physical logic: props anchored to the ground with contact shadows and a base layer, no floating buildings, no overlapping elements, and nothing placed implausibly (e.g. trees growing in the middle of a pond); reworking the composition rather than nudging coordinates. Confidence: 0.7
